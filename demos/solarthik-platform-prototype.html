Why the app looks like this

  • The source pill never says "off-grid." It says grid, solar, or battery — because the system is always meant to be read against the grid connection, not instead of it.
  • Poultry's segment card is about ventilation hours protected, not taka saved. That's the actual purchase decision for that customer.
  • Cold storage leads with the honest solar-only number — 131% coverage from panels alone, no battery cost issue to reconcile. A battery's case here is resilience (avoided spoilage, avoided diesel-generator cost), not bill arbitrage — see the disclaimer below for why that's shown separately rather than folded into one inflated percentage.
  • The outage log is deliberately visible, including partial-coverage events — transparency applied to live data, not just projections.
